## Claim

A lesson that is not tied to a later success or failure is memory, not experience. Agent-memory research treats that distinction as load-bearing. lessons.md currently has no outcome field.

## Supporting Evidence

> **Source [EXPERT]:** TsinghuaC3I Awesome-Memory-for-Agents README: Long-term memory splits Experience (knowledge explicitly validated by task outcomes) vs Memory (information without reference to task outcomes). Learning-from-Experience application = trajectories, success/failure lessons, reusable skills, cross-task transfer. https://github.com/TsinghuaC3I/Awesome-Memory-for-Agents (content_hash:33f48732), retrieved 2026-08-17

## Disconfirmation Search

Many shipping agent-memory products (Mem0, MemGPT/Letta, listed in the same survey) store unvalidated notes and call it memory. That is the dominant product pattern, so shipping notes-only is conventional. The taxonomy still separates Experience (validated by task outcome) from Memory (no outcome reference). I did not find a paper that says unvalidated notes transfer across tasks as well as outcome-tagged experience.
